## About

Risunity is a GitHub organization where we build, experiment with, and share software projects. We believe good tools and systems make life easier, so we love creating things that save time and effort.

**Mission:** Build useful, collaborative, and open work for everyone.

---

## How to Join the Organization

1. **Follow** our Instagram at [@risunity.works](https://www.instagram.com/risunity.works).
2. **Send us a DM** on Instagram with:
   - Your name
   - Your GitHub username
   - Your area of interest (e.g. Web, Mobile, AI/ML, Security, or Design)
3. **Wait for the invitation.** We'll send it to your GitHub account and email.
4. **Accept the invitation** from the email notification or at [github.com/Risunity](https://github.com/Risunity).
5. Done! You're now part of Risunity.
